## 1 — The two IC samples, and why the closeout appeared to contradict itself

The Phase 3 and Phase 4 closeouts reported, in the same message:

- a populated IC matrix, composite 1-day **−0.0562 over 43 legs**, and
- **`0 of 18 IC cells measurable`** from the new walk-forward store.

That is not a contradiction. It is two samples, and the ambiguity was that neither
surface said which one it was. **Verified numerically rather than assumed:**

| | LEGACY SELECTION HISTORY | FORWARD CROSS-SECTIONAL SAMPLE |
|---|---|---|
| source | `ledger/signals.csv` | `ledger/xsec/` |
| reader | `ic_by_date()` | `xsec_by_date()` |
| nights | **48** (2026-08-01 … 2026-09-17) | **3** (2026-09-15 … 2026-09-17) |
| rows/night | ~50 (26 / 50 / 54 / 60) | **234–235** |
| population | the top fifty **by conviction** | the whole scored cross-section |
| powers | the published IC matrix **and the publication gate** | the walk-forward report only |
| result | 43 legs · composite 1d IC −0.0562 · CI [−0.1065, −0.0060] · **NEGATIVE** | **0 of 18 cells measurable**, 2 usable 1d legs |

Three further facts, each checked against the real ledgers:

1. On all three shared nights the legacy symbols are a **strict subset** of the forward
   symbols (50 ⊂ 234, 50 ⊂ 234, 50 ⊂ 235).
2. `ic_by_date()` reads `LEDGER_CSV` and never `XSEC_DIR`; `xsec_by_date()` reads
   `XSEC_DIR` and never `LEDGER_CSV`.
3. **Three nights admit at most two one-day legs.** A 43-leg figure is therefore
   arithmetically impossible from the forward sample and can only be the legacy one.

The user's stated interpretation was correct in every particular. It is now *enforced*
rather than merely true.

## 2 — What changed, all of it labelling, regression or a corrected falsehood

**The samples are named once.** `IC_SAMPLES` holds `legacy` and `forward` with their
source, label, universe, population and caveat. `ic_matrix()` and `walkforward_report()
` take a **sample id** and derive their labels from it; the free-text `universe` parameter
that could outlive its source is gone, and an unknown id raises.

**Every published IC block names its sample.** `edge`, `ic_matrix`, `publication_gate`
and the second copy of the edge inside `index.json` all now carry `sample`,
`sample_label`, `universe` and `population`. The gate's block was previously the only
IC-bearing block in either artifact with *no* provenance at all.

**Counts, not just labels.** `ic_matrix` now records `nights`, `from`, `to` and
`rows_per_night_median`. A label can be wrong; a night count corroborates or falsifies
it, so any reader of the file alone can check the claim.

**The gate may only read the sample the board is published from.** `publication_gate()`
raises on anything but `sample == "legacy"`. Both samples produce identically shaped
cells, so a forward matrix would previously have gated the live board on two legs with
nothing noticing.

**The legacy reader refuses foreign rows.** The two ledgers share column names, so
`ic_by_date(rows)` would have accepted xsec rows and returned a fully formed matrix
labelled LEGACY. It now rejects any row carrying a `src` value.

**Each cell carries its sample.** Both files key cells by the same six signals and three
horizons and could be joined by shape without reading a root label.

**The forward study is shown, and marked.** The page fetches `ledger/walkforward.json`
and renders it directly beneath the matrix under **`FORWARD SAMPLE — INSUFFICIENT`**. The
banner is **derived** from the cells beneath it, not typed, so it lifts on the night the
sample earns it and nothing has to remember to remove it. Both files declare the
incomparability, in both directions.

**A badge that read as its opposite.** The composite row carried a badge reading
`PUBLISHED`, meaning "the signal the gate reads" — sitting beside a gate whose state is
`DIAGNOSTIC ONLY`. It reads `GATED ON` now: what it always meant, and not a verdict.

**Two corrected falsehoods, both pre-existing:**

- The Index Study provenance block rendered *"the top 50 names **by market cap**"*.
  `AUDIT-2026-09` §1.0 established from the data that the cut is a **conviction** sort and
  corrected four comments; this string — the one copy a reader actually saw — was missed.
- `edge.basis` said the coefficient was measured *"across the assets scored on both
  nights"*. It is measured across the persisted top fifty, which is a different and much
  narrower claim.

**The wording no longer overstates the population.** The gate's own `detail` asserted a
property of the whole board from a measurement taken inside its top conviction quintile.
It now names the universe, and carries `measured_on` saying so explicitly.

**Units on the cohorts.** `hit_rate_by_tier` and the regime split count **symbol-days**,
not legs — so a cohort reading MEASURED on 409 symbol-days beside eighteen IC cells
reading INSUFFICIENT is consistent, not contradictory. The unit is now on every cohort.

## 3 — The regression

`tests/test_ic_provenance.py` (17 tests) exists to stop the distinction being lost again.
It covers the four mutations that would previously have passed silently:

| mutation | what now catches it |
|---|---|
| point `ic_matrix()` at the wide sample, keep the legacy label | labels are derived from the sample id; there is no free-text parameter to leave behind |
| point `walkforward_report()` at the narrow sample | same, plus the gate refuses a non-legacy matrix |
| copy a legacy leg count into the forward report | every cell's legs must be ≤ what its own night count allows |
| let a label go stale after its source changed | artifacts are reconciled against the ledgers they name, on every validator run |

`scripts/validate_ledger.py` gains `check_ic_provenance`, which fails publication when an
IC block does not name its sample, when the gate reports a non-legacy sample, when a
matrix claims more legs than its recorded nights permit, or when the two artifacts report
the same sample id.

**The prose is under the same guard as the code.** Three of those tests read the documents
rather than the source: no document may quote a leg count without naming the sample it came
from, no document may describe the persisted universe as a market-cap cut, and the
architecture map must record both samples and both ledgers. Run over two files the
regression passed; extended to all six it failed immediately, which is what a partial guard
is worth. It found a fifth surviving copy of the market-cap falsehood — `nightly.py:2688`,
the comment describing the index control's opportunity set — four years of sibling comments
having been corrected around it, and it found that `docs/ARCHITECTURE-NOTES.md`, the
repository's file → responsibility map, contained no trace of `ledger/xsec/`,
`ledger/walkforward.json` or `ledger/perp.json`: a map that omits a file says the file does
not exist. All are now written.

**One number that looked like a third sample and is not.** `PHASE2A-CALIBRATION` §2.3
reports **47** narrow legs where the published matrix reports **43**, from the same
`ledger/signals.csv`. The calibration probe pairs every consecutive night of 48 (47 legs);
`_edge_legs()` starts at the spec boundary `_spec_breaks()` detects — 2026-08-05, the night
the median asset's score moved 36 points on a 1.21% price move — and discards the four
nights before it (44 nights, 43 legs). The difference is the boundary, not the source. That
reconciliation is now written where the 47 appears, because two unexplained leg counts on
one page is how a third sample gets invented by a later reader.
